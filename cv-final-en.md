Senior software engineer with 7 years of professional experience across very different products: fraud-prevention services, retirement, logistics, HR tools and music-related projects. This diversity taught me to understand a domain quickly, deliver the requested features and sustainably improve code readability, testing and maintainability.

I have mainly worked autonomously within small teams while acting as a technical referent: framing, trade-offs, code reviews, mentoring and coordination with product and infrastructure teams and vendors. My project-management training broadened this foundation to commercial and strategic considerations, budgeting and team leadership.

I can now also lead a virtual team of AI agents. I turn needs into specifications and acceptance criteria, delegate bounded tasks to specialized agents, isolate their work in dedicated branches, then control tests, contracts, risks and quality before integration. Agents produce and verify; I retain the decisions, technical accountability and final approval. My remote-first culture has taught me to make exchanges, decisions and documentation explicit, accessible and traceable within a shared framework for the team and AI agents, preserving context and continuity. I maintain continuous technology and industry awareness, assessing new tools and practices against real needs before adopting them.

I now want to bring my web-development experience and my practice of framing, delegation and feedback to a human team working on a well-scoped, useful product.
